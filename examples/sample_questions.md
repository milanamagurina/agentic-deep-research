# Sample research questions

The benchmark (`python -m evidence_research.evaluation.benchmark --live`) runs every numbered line below.
Each question is chosen to stress one evidence situation from the challenge brief.

1. **Sources agree** — Did the EU AI Act enter into force in August 2024, and when do its obligations for general-purpose AI models apply?
2. **Sources conflict** — How much does a typical large language model query consume in electricity compared with a web search?
3. **Many sources derive from one original** — Is the widely reported claim that 90% of online content will be AI-generated by 2026 supported by an identifiable original study?
4. **Temporally outdated evidence** — What is the current market share of Nvidia in data-center AI accelerators?
5. **Insufficient evidence** — Do four-day work week trials show a durable productivity increase after the trial period ends?

## Offline synthetic scenario

`python app.py --demo` runs a fictional case ("Does Acme Fleet have strong product-market fit?") on a scripted
corpus of `.example` domains. It contains all five situations at once: agreeing sources (revenue: press release
and registry filing), conflicting sources (retention: 95% self-reported vs 81% independent benchmark), derived
reporting (three outlets copying the press release), outdated evidence (a 2021 customer count) and insufficient
evidence (no information on acquisitions). It also contains a prompt-injection line on a review page.
