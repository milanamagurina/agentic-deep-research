# Sample research questions

`python -m evidence_research.evaluation.benchmark --live` runs every numbered line below (fast depth).

1. **Business: product-market fit** — Does Duolingo have strong product-market fit?
2. **Scientific: competing trial evidence** — Does intermittent fasting lead to more weight loss than continuous calorie restriction in adults?
3. **Sources conflict** — How much electricity does a typical large language model query use compared with a web search?
4. **Many sources derive from one original** — Is the widely repeated claim that 90% of online content will be AI-generated by 2026 traceable to an original study?
5. **Insufficient evidence** — Do four-day work week trials show a durable productivity increase after the trial period ends?
